# RideFast: Data-Driven Business Insights

Business Intelligence project leveraging Python, Pandas, and Tableau to analyze ride-sharing operations, customer behavior, and operational performance. The project transforms raw ride, user, driver, and support ticket data into actionable business insights and strategic recommendations.

## Key Insights

### Ride Fulfillment Issues

* 120,000 total ride requests
* 81.37% completion rate
* 18.63% ride failure rate

### Driver Cancellation Challenge

* Driver cancellations account for a significant portion of failed rides
* Peak demand periods show increased cancellation pressure

### Promotion Inefficiency

* Promotional rides show nearly identical completion rates compared to non-promotional rides
* Opportunities exist to optimize discount spending

### High Customer Churn

* Churn rate exceeds 79%
* Retention strategies require immediate attention

### Regional Underperformance

* Northgate records the lowest completion rate and highest driver cancellation rate among all cities
